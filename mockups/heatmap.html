Kill/death heatmap

Where you get kills and where you die, drawn over the Deadlock minimap. Three variants.

Data note: the heatmap cells are real output of /v1/analytics/kill-death-stats (all players, last 30 days, downsampled 4x4 to a 42x42 grid and embedded inline). Both API calls worked, no fake fallback needed. The data is game-wide, so the player name, totals and match count in the mockup are fake placeholders. The real feature would pass account_ids. The minimap image is hotlinked from /v1/assets/map.
