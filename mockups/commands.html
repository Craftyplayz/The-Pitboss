The Pitboss help · Getting started
What The Pitboss does
Shows Deadlock stats for this server: your Profile, Leaderboards between members, game-wide hero and item stats, and an automatic Match Recap after every game you play.
/link player:
Link your Deadlock account. Start typing your Steam name and pick yourself from the list, or paste your Steam ID or steamcommunity.com profile URL. You'll see a card with the account's profile picture: press That's me to link it. Linking again replaces your old account.
/unlink
Remove your Link. Your recaps stop and your recap post is archived.

The Pitboss help · Match Recaps
Automatic, no command needed
Once you're linked, the bot checks for your finished Matches every 5 minutes and posts a recap in your own post in the recap forum. Recaps can take 5–30 minutes to appear after a Match.
What a recap shows
K/D/A, souls, damage, healing, accuracy, crits, farm, where your souls came from, your final build, both teams' scoreboard and a souls-over-time graph.
Your past Matches
When you first link, every Match from the last 30 days gets a recap too. They post a few at a time, so a busy month can take an hour or so to fill in.
